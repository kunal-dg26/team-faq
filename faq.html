Library help desk: questions and answers

The questions students ask us most often, with short answers.

When is the library open?

The library is open from 9 am to 5 pm.

How many books can I borrow?

You can borrow up to 3 books at a time, for 14 days.

What if I return a book late?

The fine is 2 rupees for each book, for each day it is late.

Can I bring my laptop?

Yes. The charging points are next to the reading room windows.
